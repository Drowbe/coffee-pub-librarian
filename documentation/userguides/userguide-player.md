# What Players See

**Audience:** A player at a table using Librarian, and the GM deciding what to reveal.

What a player can look at, what they can do with it, and what stays hidden.

## Getting in

The **Librarian** button in the menubar is visible to everyone. Clicking it opens a bar with
**Codex** and **Quests**, and both browsers open for players.

On a fresh install both will be empty. That is expected -- nothing is visible until the GM
reveals it.

## The codex

You see the entries your GM has revealed, and no others. A hidden entry is not greyed out or
listed as locked; it is simply absent, so the codex looks smaller to you than to your GM.

For an entry you can see, you get the summary, the links, the related entries, the location,
the tags, and the full write-up behind **Read more**.

**You do not see the plot hook.** That field is GM-only on every entry, always, including on
the journal page.

You can search, filter by tag, collapse categories and expand cards. Those choices are
yours -- collapsing a category does not collapse it for anyone else.

**You cannot** add, edit, delete or reveal entries, and you will not see the controls for
those.

## Quests

You see quests your GM has revealed. Hidden quests are absent, as are individual objectives
the GM has hidden.

For each quest you can see: its description, its objectives and their states, its progress,
its reward, its timeframe and its participants.

**You do not see GM hints.** An objective written as `Search the cellar ||the key is behind
the barrel||` reads to you as "Search the cellar". The hint is invisible, and there is no
indication one exists.

## Your active objective

**Clicking an objective sets it as your active objective.** It marks what you are currently
pursuing.

It does **not** change the objective's state. Completing, failing and hiding objectives are
GM actions -- if you click one and it does not tick off, that is why, not a bug.

Click it again to clear it.

## Pins on the map

Pins for entries and quests you can see appear on the scene like any other marker, and
**double-clicking one opens it** in the browser, scrolled to the right place.

Pins for things you cannot see are not on your map at all.

## Things that will look odd

**A newly revealed entry does not appear until you reopen the codex.** If your GM says they
have just revealed something and you cannot see it, close the codex window and open it
again. This is a known defect rather than something you have done wrong -- see
[Known issues](../known-issues.md).

**Entry counts differ from your GM's.** The count at the bottom of the codex reflects what
*you* can see. A GM reading 347 while you read 40 is the module working correctly.
